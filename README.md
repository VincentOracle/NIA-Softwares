## Overview

The NIA website is the company's primary digital presence and provides information about its services, industries, approach, work, insights, company background, and contact channels.

The NIA website is designed as a modern, responsive, accessible, and performance-conscious frontend that can later be connected to a backend and administrative dashboard.

The current implementation is primarily a static website.

### Core objectives

- Present NIA' technology capabilities clearly
- Explain services and industry applications
- Showcase completed work and case studies
- Communicate NIA's approach to solving problems
- Publish insights, research, guides, and reports
- Provide clear project and consultation pathways
- Establish a foundation for future dashboard/API integration
- Maintain a premium and technically credible brand experience
