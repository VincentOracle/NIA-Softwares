WHO WE ARE

We are a technology company built around problems, not technologies.

NIA is an AI, Data & Technology Agency focused on building practical digital systems for real-world challenges. We combine software engineering, artificial intelligence, data, automation, and systems thinking to understand problems and develop solutions around them.

We are based in Kahawa, Nairobi, Kenya, and we design our work to be useful wherever complex problems exist.
