General

NIA Softwares is an AI, Data & Technology Agency focused on building practical digital solutions for real-world organizational problems. We combine artificial intelligence, software development, data, automation, and technology consulting to help organizations improve how they operate and make better decisions.

We help organizations identify technology opportunities, design practical solutions, build digital systems, integrate AI, transform data into useful intelligence, automate repetitive processes, and provide technology guidance.

Our work is designed for organizations and teams that need practical technology solutions, whether they are improving an existing process, building a new digital product, working with organizational data, introducing AI, or addressing a broader technology challenge.

NIA Softwares is based in Kahawa, Nairobi, Kenya, and can engage with organizations beyond Nairobi depending on the requirements of the project.
