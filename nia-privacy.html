1. About This Policy

NIA is an AI, Data & Technology Agency. This Privacy Policy explains how we approach personal information when you interact with our website, contact channels, and applicable digital services.

Different services may have additional privacy documentation. Where a specific product, project, or engagement involves its own privacy arrangements, those arrangements may apply in addition to this policy.

2. Information We May Collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

Depending on how you interact with NIA, you may choose to provide information such as:

  • your name;
  • email address;
  • phone or WhatsApp number;
  • organisation name;
  • project information;
  • messages and enquiries;
  • service interests;
  • other information you choose to include in a communication.

2.2 Information collected through website interaction

The NIA website is currently designed to support project enquiry preparation and general browsing. Where website features generate or process information, this policy will be updated to reflect those features as they are introduced.

2.3 Information provided through third-party channels

When you use a third-party channel such as WhatsApp or email to communicate with NIA, information you provide through that channel may be processed according to that provider's own terms and privacy practices.

3. How Information Is Collected

NIA may receive information through channels such as:

  • project enquiry preparation on the website;
  • direct WhatsApp communication;
  • email;
  • website interactions;
  • project engagements;
  • other channels you choose to use.

Only channels that are actually used will apply to your interaction.

4. How We Use Information

Personal information may be used for purposes such as:

  • responding to enquiries;
  • communicating with prospective clients;
  • understanding project requirements;
  • discussing potential services;
  • providing requested information;
  • providing applicable services;
  • managing client relationships;
  • improving website functionality where relevant;
  • maintaining website security where relevant;
  • complying with applicable obligations.

Personal information should be used for appropriate purposes connected to the interaction or service for which it was provided, subject to applicable law.

5. Contact Requests & WhatsApp

At present, NIA uses WhatsApp as a primary channel for project enquiries. Information entered into the website project-intake interface is used to prepare a message that you can review and send through WhatsApp.

You choose whether to send the message. NIA does not receive the information merely because it was entered on the website.

A project enquiry message may include information such as:

  • your name;
  • contact details;
  • organisation;
  • selected services;
  • project description;
  • timeline;
  • budget information if you choose to provide it.

Once you choose to send a message through WhatsApp, the communication is also subject to WhatsApp's applicable terms and privacy practices. NIA does not control WhatsApp's processing.
